If I had to give 2025 a headline, it would probably be something like:
**"The Year of Realignment."**

Not the flashy type of reinvention, but the quieter, more grounded kind—the one where you finally listen to yourself, make space for what matters, and occasionally fall on your face (sometimes literally… looking at you, wakeboarding).
It was a year where I built better habits, chased passions, learned lessons the hard way, and rediscovered parts of myself I didn't know were waiting.

Here's the journey.

<!--more-->

***

## 🌱 **The Theme: A Year of Realignment**

2025 was the year I put my mental and physical wellbeing at the center of the table—not off to the side, not squeezed between work and errands, but right in the middle where it belongs.

I doubled down on **mental health**, spent a surprising amount of time **reflecting on my values**, and experimented with new ways to slow down and be present. At the same time, I gave more space to creativity—especially music—while trying new sports and revisiting old ones.

There was growth, absolutely… but also a feeling of "coming back home" to myself.

***

## 🌟 **The Highlights**

### 🎧 Growing My DJ Journey

This was a big one.

*   I **completed the Crossfader DJ course** for my DDJ‑FLX4, and it genuinely leveled up my mixing confidence.
*   I played my **first public, paid DJ event at the Microsoft AI Tour**—a milestone I won't forget.
*   And on April 4th, during our Friday Bar at Microsoft, I made my colleagues dance to my set as we celebrated the company's 50th birthday. Definitely one of those "wow, I get to do this?" moments.
*   On the geekier side, I **built a custom tool to simplify library management** for Rekordbox + Mixed In Key users. It grew out of a personal pain point and turned into a full vibe‑coding experiment in VS Code—one of my favorite AI‑assisted dev stories of the year. (Blog link coming soon.)

### ✈️ Following the Music—Literally

I followed one of my favorite DJ groups, **Above & Beyond**, on their European tour, hopping between **Amsterdam**, **Lausanne**, and **Copenhagen**. Three cities, three unique energies, one very happy version of me.
And speaking of unforgettable music experiences:
for the second time in my life, two years after my first attendance, I returned to Anjunadeep Open Air London 2025, and it was nothing short of sensational. The event took place at the iconic Old Royal Naval College in Greenwich, a setting that already feels cinematic on its own, but becomes downright magical when paired with deep, melodic electronic music. From 2PM until 10PM, I got to listen to some of my favorite artists as the Canary Wharf skyline painted a stunning backdrop. It was a full‑day journey through rhythms, emotions, and unforgettable vibes. Truly one of those "I"m so grateful to be here" moments.

### 🧠 Prioritizing My Mental Health

This was probably the biggest overarching theme of 2025.

I invested intentionally in:

*   **Therapy** (shout‑out to Spring Health),
*   The **Headspace** app,
*   Work‑related wellbeing learning paths (burnout, resilience, self‑care),
*   And a whole mini‑library of audiobooks and ebooks:
    *   *Why We Sleep*
    *   *Atomic Habits*
    *   *Four Thousand Weeks*
    *   *The Let Them Theory*

Together, they became a kind of mental reset button.
And yes—AI played a surprising role in some of the self‑reflection exercises. (Future historians: please document this era correctly.)

### 🌍 Summer Escapes: Canary Islands & Dolomites

2025 also gave me some truly memorable holiday moments.
First stop: **Fuerteventura**, which also marked my first time in the Canary Islands.
I got lost (in the good way) between surreal landscapes and wild coasts:
the massive **Corralejo Dunes**, the striking cliffs of **Playa de la Pared**, the isolated beauty of **Cofete**, the relaxed charm of **El Cotillo**, the black‑sand caves of **Ajuy**, the quiet old streets of **Betancuria**, and the beachside vibes of **Morro Jable**. Fuerteventura surprised me with its mix of desert, volcanoes, wind, and raw Atlantic energy — a quiet different yet characteristic rhythm of life, that I'm not sure I would easily get used to.

Later in the summer, after almost 20 years, I returned to the Dolomites — and it felt like rediscovering a childhood memory in HD.
From the postcard‑perfect **Ortisei** and **Alpe di Siusi**, to wandering around **Bolzano**, to two breathtaking hikes: the dramatic peaks of the **Odle** and the surreal, almost sci‑fi scenery of **Cadini di Misurina**. I wrapped it all up with a peaceful stop at the beautiful **Lago di Dobbiaco**, one of those places where the world feels unusually calm for a while.
These trips reminded me how much I love landscapes that make you feel small in the best possible way.

### 💪 Moving My Body Again

2025 was the year I remembered that my body can do cool things... if I actually ask it to.

*   I tried **Yoga** for the first time and started incorporating mobility + flexibility work way more consistently.
*   I spent the summer trying to land a **wakeboard tantrum**. Spoiler: I didn't land a single one. But the attempt counts (I'm pretty sure?).
*   And in a fun twist of nostalgia, I put on skis after **20 years**—and somehow discovered I can still ski decently well. Muscle memory is wild.

### 🧾 Becoming a Responsible Adult (Finally)

After years of procrastination, confusion, and existential dread, I finally got my **Danish taxes** in proper order.
A deeply unsexy achievement, but a huge weight lifted.

## 😬 **The Honest Part**

I learned the hard way what it means to **lose your mobile phone while on vacation** in 2025.
Spoiler: it's awful. Our lives orbit around that tiny device way more than they should, and being suddenly disconnected was both stressful and strangely eye‑opening. A modern‑age low‑light with a valuable lesson buried inside.

## 🔭 **The Horizon: Looking Toward 2026**

If 2025 was about realignment, then 2026 feels like the year to build on that foundation.

I want to:

*   Keep expanding my DJ presence with more gigs and more original content.
*   Continue deepening the wellbeing habits that made such a difference.
*   Reinvest time in the sports and activities that reconnect me with my body.
*   Learn the tantrum. No seriously—I'm not giving up.
*   Explore more AI‑driven creativity, especially in music and coding.

Most importantly: I want 2026 to be intentional, joyful, and grounded.
